# Code of Conduct

Outspoken exists to give a voice to people who have lost theirs. We want working on it to be welcoming for everyone, whatever their background, identity, ability or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect the people the app is for. Describe disability, illness and care plainly and with dignity.
- Protect privacy. Never post logs, recordings, screenshots or details that identify a patient, a carer or anyone near the phone without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile, [@DarkBird10020](https://github.com/DarkBird10020). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
