# Security and privacy policy

Outspoken runs entirely on the phone and has no internet permission. Even so, it handles very private moments: what a person in care says, and what is said around them. We take reports seriously.

## What counts

- Anything that could let the app send data off the phone (for example a library that brings the INTERNET permission back).
- Run logs or files readable by other apps, or kept somewhere unexpected.
- A way for the app to say something the person did not choose, or to miss a help alarm.
- A damaged or substituted model file being loaded.

## How to report

Please do not open a public issue for a security or privacy problem. Report it privately through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/DarkBird10020/outspoken/security/advisories/new), or contact the maintainers through [@DarkBird10020](https://github.com/DarkBird10020).

Include the build (shown on the Model screen), the phone and Android version, and the steps to reproduce. Leave out anything a person said: describe it instead.

We aim to reply within a few days and will credit you in the fix unless you would rather not be named.

## Supported versions

Only the latest build from `main` is supported.
