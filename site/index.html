How it works

A whole conversation, with only the eyes.

No switches, no head pointer, no laptop. One phone on a stand in front of the person.

1

It listens

The visitor speaks as normal. The phone hears them, writes it in the Heard card, and Gemma, running on the phone, writes four replies that fit the person and the moment.

2

They look

A look up moves the highlight to the next card. The app learns where the eyes rest at every start, so it works with the phone wherever it sits.

3

They blink

A deliberate close of about half a second says the lit card out loud, in a natural voice. Ordinary blinks are ignored.

The app

Calm screens, built for tired eyes.

Big cards, one highlight, soft light. Every screen reads from across a hospital bed.

Features

Everything a bedside conversation needs.

Gemma on the phone

Gemma 4 runs on the phone through LiteRT-LM and writes four replies in about a second. It knows the person from their profile: their name, family, likes and conditions.

Say anything

When no card fits, build any sentence a few words at a time. The model suggests the next words and a finished sentence.

Help alarm

Eyes shut for 2 seconds, then a confirm blink, sounds an alarm and texts and calls the carer. Sleep cannot set it off.

A natural voice

Kokoro, a neural voice that runs on the phone, speaks the cards. Replies are voiced while the person chooses, so nothing waits.

Calibrates itself

A short calibration at every start measures this person's eyes, so blink and look lines fit them, glasses and all.

17 languages

Cards, voice and listening follow whichever language the visitor speaks.

Lessons and practice

Step-by-step lessons for the person and the carer, and practice rounds with the camera for blinking, moving and calling for help.

Hand signs

For people who can lift a hand: thumbs up says yes, fingers light a card, a fist says it.

Patient profile

Name, photo, the people close to them, likes and allergies go into every reply, on the phone only.

Live session stats

Reply time, model speed, blink accuracy and the phone's temperature, live while you talk.

Privacy

What is said at the bedside stays at the bedside.

The app has no internet permission at all. The model, the voice, the listening and the eye tracking all run on the phone. The build checks this on every change.

  • No internet permission, ever
  • No accounts, no cloud, no sync
  • Conversations live in memory only
  • Free and open source, Apache 2.0

Download the APK

Android 12 or later

  1. Download the APK on the Android phone.
  2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
  3. Download the model from the app's Model screen, once, over Wi-Fi (about 2.6 GB). After that the app never needs the internet.
  4. Put the phone on a stand in front of the person and follow the short calibration.
